Research Interests

  • Rigidity and arithmetic properties of local systems.
  • Moduli stacks and deformation-theoretic methods for studying local systems.
  • Complex, ℓ-adic, and p-adic local systems.
  • ℓ-independence.
  • p-adic Hodge theory.
  • Fontaine–Mazur conjecture.
  • Simpson’s motivicity conjecture.
